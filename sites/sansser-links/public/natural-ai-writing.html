Prompt guide

Make your AI writing sound like you

Copy these into ChatGPT, Claude or whatever you use. Start with the main prompt, then use the fix-it prompts on anything that still sounds off.

1

The main prompt (use this every time)

Write this in plain, natural English, the way a real person would explain it to a friend. Use punctuation normally and avoid em dashes unless one is genuinely needed. Don't set up points as contrasts like "it's not X, it's Y". Avoid filler phrases such as "delve into", "unlock the power of", "in today's fast-paced world", "game-changer" and "let's dive in". Don't repeat a point once it's made, and don't add a summary at the end. Only use lists or headings if they make the information easier to follow. If you're unsure of a detail, ask me instead of making it up.
Here's what I want to say: [your topic, your opinion, any examples or experiences]
2

Teach it your voice

Here are a few things I've written myself: [paste 2–3 samples]. Look at how I write: sentence length, word choice, how I open and close, how casual I am. Describe my style back to me in a few lines, then write [your task] in that style. Don't copy my sentences, just match the way I write.
3

Give it something real to say

Before you write anything, ask me 5 short questions about my experience, opinions and examples on [topic]. Wait for my answers, then write the piece using only what I tell you. Don't add generic advice or claims I didn't make.
4

Fix the punctuation

Go through this text and remove em dashes that interrupt the flow. Replace them with a comma, a full stop or a rewrite, whichever reads most naturally. Keep any that genuinely work. Don't change anything else.

Break the patterns

Find every sentence in this text built as a contrast or a formula, like "it's not X, it's Y" or "not just X, but Y". Rewrite each one to state the point directly. Leave the rest as it is.

6

Remove AI vocabulary

7

Cut the repetition

8

Loosen the structure

Rewrite this so the structure fits the content instead of a template. Turn lists back into sentences unless the list genuinely helps, drop headings that aren't needed and remove the wrap-up conclusion if it just repeats things.

9

The read-aloud check
